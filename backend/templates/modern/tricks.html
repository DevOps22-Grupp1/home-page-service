The tricks, explained

Why a shop full of tricks?

Scamazon uses {{ tricks | length }} dark patterns: design that nudges you into doing what the shop wants rather than what you meant to do. Real shops use the same ones, usually less obviously.

Here they are on purpose, so you can see them work. Press Reveal the tricks in the menu and every trick on a page gets a dashed red outline and a number.

How to spot them

  • A countdown that never seems to end, or starts over when you reload.
  • Numbers about other shoppers that you cannot check.
  • A "was" price you never saw the product sell for.
  • Ticked boxes you did not tick, and costs that appear at the end.
  • A "no" that is worded to make you feel silly.
